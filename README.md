## About

Astro is an AI-powered email client that brings machine learning to email management. Features AI-assisted email prioritization, smart unsubscribe recommendations, automatic email snoozing, and scheduled send. Astro's AI layer helps triage busy inboxes by learning from user behavior to surface important messages and minimize inbox noise for productivity-focused professionals.

## Common Problems & Limitations

- **Platform Availability** — Limited platform support vs mainstream email clients
- **AI Dependency** — Core value requires active AI model training on your email patterns
- **Privacy Considerations** — AI email processing requires access to full email content
- **Subscription Model** — AI features require paid subscription
